    One night only

    1. 1970s
      Pelé, Cruyff, Beckenbauer

      The NASL era brought the world's greatest to Toronto, Vancouver and Montreal.

    2. 1983
      Michel Platini

      Juventus and the three-time Ballon d'Or winner faced the Toronto Blizzard.

    3. 1996
      Diego Maradona

      Played an exhibition for Toronto Italia alongside his brother.

    4. 2007
      Agüero, Di María, Suárez, Piqué

      The U-20 World Cup in Canada. Agüero won the Golden Ball and Golden Boot as Argentina lifted the trophy.

    5. 2009
      Cristiano Ronaldo

      Real Madrid beat Toronto FC 5–1 in a friendly at Rogers Centre.

    6. May 2026
      Lionel Messi

      A goal and two assists as Inter Miami won 4–2 at BMO Field, in front of a record 44,828.

    7. Jul 2026
      Cristiano Ronaldo

      Scored a penalty at BMO Field as Portugal beat Croatia 2–1, becoming the oldest scorer in World Cup knockout history (41).
