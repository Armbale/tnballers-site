THE GREATS PLAYED HERE.

From Pelé to Messi, the biggest names in the game have played on Canadian grass. Some of them still call it home.

Playing here now

Thomas Müller

Vancouver Whitecaps · Since Aug 2025

2014 World Cup winner. 13 Bundesliga titles with Bayern. Now in Vancouver: 9 goals and 6 assists in MLS this season (currently out with a hamstring injury).

Playing here now

Alexis Sánchez

CF Montréal · Since Aug 2026

Chile's all-time top scorer (51) and most-capped player (168). Two Copa Américas, and title winner at Barcelona and Inter.

They called Canada home

  • 🇮🇹2015–19

    Sebastian Giovinco

    Toronto FC

    MLS MVP in his first season. Led TFC to the 2017 treble: MLS Cup, Supporters' Shield and Canadian Championship.

  • 🇨🇮2015–16

    Didier Drogba

    Montreal Impact

    Chelsea's Champions League hero hit a hat-trick on his first start and 11 goals in his first 11 MLS games.

  • 🇫🇷2019–21

    Thierry Henry

    CF Montréal (head coach)

    Arsenal's all-time top scorer and a World Cup winner coached Montreal through the 2020 season.

  • 🇮🇹2012–13

    Alessandro Nesta

    Montreal Impact

    2006 World Cup winner and two-time Champions League winner, finishing his career in Montreal.

  • 🇮🇹2022–25

    Lorenzo Insigne

    Toronto FC

    Euro 2020 winner with Italy. Napoli legend turned Toronto FC designated player.

PRIVACY POLICY.

Effective 17 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information True North Ballers ("TNB", "we", "us") collects through tnballers.com, why, and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and treat visitors from elsewhere, including the UK and EU, the same way.

Who is responsible

True North Ballers, Brampton, Ontario, Canada. Send questions or requests to contact@tnballers.com.

What we collect

  • Get Scouted submissions: your name, email, position, age group, current club, what you tell us about yourself, a parent or guardian's email if you're under 18, and the video file or link you send.
  • Book a Shoot requests: your name, email, phone number if you give it, who you are, what you need filmed, the date, location and budget, and any details you add. We use these only to reply to and plan your shoot.
  • Emails you send us: whatever the message contains.
  • Technical data: when you load the site, our host (Netlify) and font provider (Google Fonts) receive your IP address, browser type and the time of the request, as any web server does. We don't use this to identify you.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, tracking pixels or cookies. See the Cookie Statement.

Why we use it

  • To review your submission and decide whether to follow up.
  • To contact you, and your parent or guardian if you're under 18, about coverage or opportunities.
  • To answer your messages, and to keep the site running and secure.

We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit the form. We will ask you again before we publish any footage or story about you, and get a parent or guardian's consent if you're under 18. We only share your details with a club or scout if you agree first.

Young players

Many players who contact us are under 18. If you are, we need a parent or guardian's email and will contact them before we follow up with you or publish anything. Children under 13 should not use the form; a parent or guardian can email us instead.

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent personal information. These providers process data for us:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States): website hosting and storage of form submissions and uploaded files.
  • Google LLC (United States): serves the website's fonts. Google receives your IP address but sets no cookies.
  • Our email provider, for messages you send us.

These providers may store data outside Canada, where it is subject to local law, including lawful access by authorities there.

Players featured on this site

The Players pages show professional information: names, clubs, positions, publicly reported match statistics, honours, public social handles and photos. Players appear with their agreement. Featured players can ask us to change or remove anything at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep scouting submissions for up to 24 months after we last hear from you, then delete them. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing contact@tnballers.com. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (opens in new tab), or, in the UK or EU, your local data protection authority such as the Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and submissions are kept in a password-protected account only TNB can access. Please don't send sensitive information such as health details or ID numbers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and highlight significant changes on the site.

ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT.

Effective 17 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use tnballers.com, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and controls meet AA colour contrast.
  • Everything works with a keyboard. Tab moves between controls, Enter or Space activates them, and Escape closes player cards and the menu. On the pitch, Tab to a player and press Enter to open their card.
  • A "Skip to content" link appears on the first Tab press.
  • Images have text alternatives, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • The interactive pitch has a full text alternative on the Players & Stats page.
  • Moving content can be paused with the pause button in the top bar, and animations switch off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Form fields have labels and clear error messages.

Feedback

If something doesn't work for you, email contact@tnballers.com with the page and the problem and we'll help you get the information another way.
