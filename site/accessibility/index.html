Accessibility.

We target WCAG 2.1 AA on tnballers.com, in both the dark and light themes.

What that means in practice

  • Body-text contrast is 4.5:1 or higher against its background, in both themes.
  • Every interactive element is keyboard-reachable with a visible focus ring.
  • Images have alt text. Decorative icons are marked aria-hidden="true".
  • Animations and the live ticker respect your system's "Reduce motion" preference and switch to a static layout when it is on.
  • Player cards and profile pop-ups are reachable by Tab, open on Enter and Space, close on Escape, and move between players with the Left and Right arrow keys.
  • Forms have proper labels, visible focus states, and don't rely on colour alone to signal state.
  • The theme toggle lives in the top bar; it also respects your system prefers-color-scheme on first visit.

Report a problem

If any part of the site is hard to use with your assistive tech, email contact@tnballers.com and we'll fix it.
