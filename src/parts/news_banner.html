🏆 Champions

Forge FC are the 2026 Canadian Champions. They beat CF Montréal 4–2 on penalties after a 1–1 draw at Stade Saputo, becoming the first CPL club ever to win it, and earned a place in the 2027 CONCACAF Champions Cup. Congratulations to TNB's Kevaughn Tavernier and Zayne Bruno and the whole Forge squad.
